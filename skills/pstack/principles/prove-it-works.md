# Prove It Works

**Trigger:** Use before declaring any task complete.

Exercise the exact artifact through the real behavior surface and capture reproducible evidence. Compilation, a green proxy test, CI status, or an agent statement is insufficient unless it is the contracted surface. Bind the verdict to the tested fingerprint and invalidate it after edits.

## Application record

When this principle materially changes a decision, record the concrete choice and evidence in `pstack_decision`. Do not cite a principle as decoration; state what alternative it ruled out or what structure it introduced.
