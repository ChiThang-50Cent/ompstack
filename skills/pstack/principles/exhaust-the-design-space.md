# Exhaust the Design Space

**Trigger:** Use for novel interactions or architecture with no strong precedent.

Generate two or three meaningfully different candidates, not cosmetic variants. Freeze a rubric and build enough of each to reveal tradeoffs. Select by evidence and constraints. Stop exploring when additional candidates no longer change the decision.

## Application record

When this principle materially changes a decision, record the concrete choice and evidence in `pstack_decision`. Do not cite a principle as decoration; state what alternative it ruled out or what structure it introduced.
