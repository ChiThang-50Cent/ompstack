# Operator: Interrogate an artifact

Use when a design or patch is contested, high-risk, or needs multiple independent reviews.

Give every reviewer:
- the same frozen intent and non-goals;
- the actual artifact/diff and base;
- the same rubric and output schema;
- relevant environment and acceptance;
- no builder self-justification unless it is itself under review.

Diversity should come from independent contexts/models, not arbitrary role-play. Require concrete location, trigger, impact, and evidence. The coordinator deduplicates by root cause and classifies findings as fix, consider, note, or reject with a reason. After edits, prior artifact-bound review conclusions become stale in changed areas.
