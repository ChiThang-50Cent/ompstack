# Never Block on the Human

**Trigger:** Use before asking for guidance on reversible work.

Proceed with safe local investigation, prototypes, tests, and reasonable reversible defaults. Resolve observable questions by running or measuring. Ask only for inaccessible information, genuine preference/value decisions not covered by goals, or irreversible/high-consequence authorization.

## Application record

When this principle materially changes a decision, record the concrete choice and evidence in `pstack_decision`. Do not cite a principle as decoration; state what alternative it ruled out or what structure it introduced.
